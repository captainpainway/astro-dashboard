MOON TEXTURE  (src/assets/moon-2k.jpg)
--------------------------------------
NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit. LROC colour map built from
Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter data. NASA media are generally not copyrighted.
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720


BACKGROUND IMAGE  (src/assets/webb-background.webp)
---------------------------------------------------

LUNAR FEATURES  (src/engine/lunar.json)
---------------------------------------
Feature names and coordinates are from the USGS / IAU Gazetteer of Planetary
Nomenclature, Moon. https://planetarynames.wr.usgs.gov


WIKIPEDIA SUMMARIES  (src/engine/wikiSummaries.json)
----------------------------------------------------
The short descriptions of targets and lunar features are extracts of Wikipedia
articles, fetched by scripts/fetch-wiki-summaries.mjs and bundled with the app.
Text is available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Each summary links to its
article, where the authors are listed. Article photos are loaded from Wikimedia
at view time and carry their own licences, shown on each file's Wikimedia page.
